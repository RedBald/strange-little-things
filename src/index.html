About Lisa

Lisa has always loved the simple, creative parts of life. She enjoys knitting, coloring, spending time with family, caring for animals, and collecting anything cute, unusual, or full of personality.

She married at 21 and later stepped away from work to focus on raising her two sons. Family life took her in directions she never expected. She went from living on a small farm with chickens, turkeys, geese, goats, and plenty of dogs to becoming a full-time band parent and wheelchair basketball mom.

For years, her schedule revolved around practices, games, tournaments, school events, medical appointments, and making sure her boys had every opportunity possible. She rarely missed an event and proudly supported both sons through graduation.

Now a recent empty nester, Lisa lives with her husband, an IT nerd who somehow keeps all the technology working. With her sons grown and a little more time for herself, she began discovering new hobbies and reconnecting with the creative things she had always enjoyed.

For most of her life, Lisa believed she couldn’t keep a plant alive if her life depended on it. Once the boys moved out, something changed. Plants suddenly started making sense, and caring for them became natural, relaxing, and rewarding.

Air plants quickly became her favorite. They’re easy to care for, endlessly interesting, and each one seems to have its own strange little shape and personality. They’ve also become some of the most popular plants she offers, making them the strangest and most lovable of her little things.

Lisa loves dogs, handmade creations, whimsical decorations, mushrooms, tiny treasures, soft colors, fantasy-inspired designs, and anything that makes a space feel warmer or more personal. She believes ordinary things become special when they’re chosen, grown, or created with care.

Her little shop is a collection of the things she loves most: plants, creativity, family, nature, and strange little finds that make people smile.
